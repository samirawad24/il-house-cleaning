Move-in and move-out cleaning

A move clean is for an empty home. We clean inside the cabinets, drawers, closets, oven and fridge, along with everything in a deep clean. A move clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in the Naperville area costs $410 to $475.

Who books it

Tenants who want the home ready for the final walkthrough, buyers who want a clean start before the furniture arrives, and landlords and realtors turning a home between people. You don't need to be there. Leave a lockbox code and we'll text you when we're done.

Prices

The price depends on bedrooms, bathrooms and square footage. A team of two spends about 4.5 hours on a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home.

Questions people ask

Call or text (630) 555-0142 if yours isn't here.

Does the home need to be empty?

Mostly. We can work around a few boxes, but we can't clean inside cabinets or closets that are still full, and we don't move furniture.

Will a move-out clean get my deposit back?

We clean to the standard most Illinois leases ask for, and we come back within 24 hours if your walkthrough turns up something we missed. Your landlord still makes the call on the deposit.

Can you clean on closing day?

Often, yes. Book the earliest arrival window and tell us your closing time in the booking notes.
