Standard house cleaning

We dust and wipe the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, vacuum and mop the floors, and take out the trash. A standard clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in the Naperville area costs $220 to $255.

Who it's for

Homes that get cleaned every few weeks, or homes in decent shape that need a reset. If nobody has cleaned in a couple of months, book a deep clean first. The standard clean keeps up after that.

What's included

Kitchen

  • Wipe counters and backsplash
  • Clean the stovetop and drip pans
  • Clean the outside of the fridge, oven, dishwasher and microwave
  • Clean the microwave inside
  • Scrub and shine the sink and faucet
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
  • Empty the trash and put in a new liner
  • Vacuum and mop the floor

Bathrooms

  • Scrub the toilet inside, outside and around the base
  • Clean the tub, shower walls and glass
  • Clean the sink, counter and faucet
  • Clean mirrors
  • Wipe cabinet fronts
  • Empty the trash
  • Vacuum and mop the floor

Bedrooms

  • Dust nightstands, dressers and shelves
  • Make the beds, and change sheets if you leave clean ones out
  • Vacuum carpet and mop hard floors
  • Empty the trash

Living areas

  • Dust shelves, decor and TV stands
  • Clean glass tables and mirrors
  • Wipe door handles and light switches
  • Clean salt and mud marks at the entry
  • Vacuum and mop floors and stairs

Not included in any cleaning

  • Outside windows and anything that needs a ladder taller than a step stool

  • Mold remediation, pest droppings or bodily fluids
  • Carpet shampooing and upholstery steam cleaning
  • Pet waste, litter boxes and cages

Questions people ask

How often should I book a standard clean?

Most families with kids or pets book every two weeks. Couples and people living alone often book every four weeks. Weekly visits cost 20% less per visit and keep the house close to spotless.

Do I need a deep clean first?

Only if the home hasn't had a professional clean in the last couple of months. Pick "It's been a while" in the price calculator and we price the extra time in.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. The team brings vacuums, mops, cloths and products. If you want us to use your own products, leave them out and tell us in the booking notes.
