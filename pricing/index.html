How we figure your price

Every price starts from the same formula, so two homes of the same size pay the same.

  • $80 to start, plus $22 per bedroom, $30 per full bathroom and $15 per half bathroom.
  • 3.5 cents for each square foot over 1,000.
  • A deep clean costs 1.6 times a standard clean. A move-in or move-out clean costs 1.85 times.
  • Homes that are a bit behind cost 15% more. Homes where it's been a while cost 30% more.
  • Pets add $15 for the extra time on hair.
  • The smallest cleaning we book is $129.

Paying and cancelling

We charge your card after the cleaning is done. No deposit to book.

Reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours before your arrival window at no charge. Inside 48 hours we charge a $50 fee.

What no cleaning includes

  • Moving heavy furniture or appliances
  • Mold remediation, pest droppings or bodily fluids
  • Carpet shampooing and upholstery steam cleaning
  • Pet waste, litter boxes and cages
