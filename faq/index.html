House cleaning questions and answers

Prices, booking, what's included and what happens on cleaning day. Call (630) 555-0142 if your question isn't here.

All questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Naperville?

A standard clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home of about 1,800 square feet costs $220 to $255. A deep clean of the same home costs $355 to $410. Recurring visits cost 10% to 20% less. The price calculator gives you an exact figure for your home.

Do I need to be home?

You don't need to be home. Most customers leave a door code or a lockbox key.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We bring vacuums, mops, cloths and products. Leave your own out if you'd rather we use them.

How long does a cleaning take?

A team of two spends about 2.5 hours on a standard clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, and about 4 hours on a deep clean. The price calculator shows the time for your home.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Yes. Tell us about your pets in the calculator so we bring extra time for pet hair. Please keep pets that don't like visitors in a closed room.

What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back to fix it at no charge.

How do I pay?

We charge your card after the cleaning is done. No deposit to book.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours before your arrival window at no charge. Inside 48 hours we charge a $50 fee.

Do you clean finished basements?

Yes. Add a finished basement in the calculator for $55. It covers a family room, a bathroom and the stairs.

What do you do about winter salt and slush?

We clean the salt lines and mud off entry floors and mats on every visit from November to March. If a snowstorm closes the roads, we call you to reschedule at no charge.

Are your cleaners insured?

Yes. We carry liability insurance and our team is bonded.

Do you clean apartments and condos?

Yes. Use the same price calculator and enter your square footage.

How often should I book a standard clean?

Most families with kids or pets book every two weeks. Couples and people living alone often book every four weeks. Weekly visits cost 20% less per visit and keep the house close to spotless.

Do I need a deep clean first?

Only if the home hasn't had a professional clean in the last couple of months. Pick "It's been a while" in the price calculator and we price the extra time in.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. The team brings vacuums, mops, cloths and products. If you want us to use your own products, leave them out and tell us in the booking notes.

What's the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean keeps a home in good shape. A deep clean adds baseboards, door frames, light switches, vents, fans, grout and the range hood, and spends longer on buildup in the kitchen and bathrooms. It takes about 1.6 times as long.

How long does a deep clean take?

For a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, a team of two spends about 4 hours. Bigger homes and homes that need extra work take longer.

Is the inside of the oven and fridge included?

Not in a deep clean. Add them in the price calculator for $35 and $35. Both come included with a move-in or move-out clean.

Does the home need to be empty?

Mostly. We can work around a few boxes, but we can't clean inside cabinets or closets that are still full, and we don't move furniture.

Will a move-out clean get my deposit back?

We clean to the standard most Illinois leases ask for, and we come back within 24 hours if your walkthrough turns up something we missed. Your landlord still makes the call on the deposit.

Can you clean on closing day?

Often, yes. Book the earliest arrival window and tell us your closing time in the booking notes.

Can I skip or move a visit?

Yes. Tell us 48 hours before your arrival window and we'll move it or skip it at no charge.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. You can stop any time with 48 hours notice.

What does the first visit cost?

If the home hasn't had a professional clean in a while, we start with a deep clean at the deep clean price. The discounted recurring price starts with the second visit.
