About Prairie Home Cleaning

We're a house cleaning company based in Naperville, Illinois. We clean homes in Naperville and 7 towns around it, and we publish our prices so you know the cost before anyone walks through your door.

How we work

You see the price before you book, and that's the price you pay unless you add something on the day. The team arrives in your arrival window, works from the same checklist in every home, and texts you when they're done.

  • Insured and bonded

    Our liability coverage protects your home while we work.

  • We bring supplies

    Vacuums, mops, cloths and products come with us.

  • 24-hour re-clean

    Missed a spot? Tell us within a day and we come back for free.

  • Text before we arrive

    You get a text when the team is on the way.

Our policies

We charge your card after the cleaning is done. No deposit to book.

Reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours before your arrival window at no charge. Inside 48 hours we charge a $50 fee.

You don't need to be home. Most customers leave a door code or a lockbox key.
