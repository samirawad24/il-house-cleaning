Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-17

This page explains what Prairie Home Cleaning does with the information you give us on this website.

What we collect

When you use the price calculator or send a booking or contact request, we collect what you type: your name, phone number, email, address and details about your home. The price calculator itself runs in your browser and sends nothing until you submit a request.

How we use it

We use your details to give you a price, schedule and confirm your cleaning, send reminders and answer your questions. We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Booking tools

Our online calendar runs on Cal.com, which stores your booking and sends confirmation emails. Our service area map loads from Google Maps. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Keeping or deleting your information

We keep customer records for as long as you book with us and for tax purposes after that. Call or email us and we'll delete your information or send you a copy.
